How we work with your team

Scope the shoot

Share your treatment, brand guidelines, shoot city and preferred dates. We define the local crew, locations, schedule and deliverables with you before production.

Produce locally

We coordinate the agreed local crew and shoot logistics. For a remote creative team, we can agree on who reviews selects or key setups and when feedback is needed.

Review and deliver

We agree on review rounds and file handover before the shoot. Deliverables can include edited stills, a hero film, cutdowns, captions and channel-specific exports.

Send a project brief

Include the brand, shoot city, dates, scope, budget range and currency. If you have a treatment, shot list or delivery specs, include a link.
